Beispiel B – typischer LLM-Stil (glatt, aufzählend, hochwertig)

Maintaining a bicycle in good working condition requires regular attention to several key components. First, it is important to inspect the tires for proper inflation and signs of wear, as under-inflated tires can lead to reduced efficiency and increased risk of punctures. Second, the chain should be cleaned and lubricated periodically to ensure smooth shifting and to prevent premature wear. Finally, brake pads should be checked regularly, as worn pads can significantly compromise stopping power and rider safety.

The architects solved the problem by cutting a row of skylights into the roof and lowering the reading tables so that the light fell directly onto them in the afternoon. Older residents still remember the smell of the sacks that had been stored in the cellar for decades, and for the first few winters the heating struggled to keep the upper gallery warm. The collection grew slowly at first, mostly through donations from farmers' families and a retired schoolteacher who left more than two thousand books in her will. By the late seventies the library had added a small music room, a children's corner with a painted ceiling, and a local history archive that is still used by students writing about the region today.
