Maintaining a bicycle in good working condition requires regular attention to several key components. First, it is important to inspect the tires for proper inflation and signs of wear, as under-inflated tires can lead to reduced efficiency and increased risk of punctures. Second, the chain should be cleaned and lubricated periodically to ensure smooth shifting and to prevent premature wear. Finally, brake pads should be checked regularly, as worn pads can significantly compromise stopping power and rider safety.
