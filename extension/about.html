  • Blocklist (generated/blocklist.js, "never scan"): compiled from UT1 blacklists (Université Toulouse Capitole, CC BY-SA 4.0), FDIC and NCUA (public domain, US agencies) and Wikidata (CC0). Because of the UT1 parts the list as a whole is under CC BY-SA 4.0.
