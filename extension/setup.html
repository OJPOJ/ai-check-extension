A hint, not proof:
  • Human texts get marked red too – especially factual, formulaic or very smoothly edited ones (encyclopedia articles, press releases, how-tos).
  • Not a probability. "AI score 97" does not mean "97% by an AI"; the number is not calibrated.
  • Not a judgment about people. Please do not accuse anyone of using AI on this basis alone.
  • English only, short texts are not scored automatically, revised AI text and newer models are detected less well.

You're set – how to use it

  1. Pin the extension (puzzle icon in the toolbar) so the icon is always one click away.
  2. Open an article, click the icon and press Scan page now (or Alt+Shift+S). Paragraphs get a green, yellow or red mark with a score.
  3. Click a mark to see the score and why it is only a hint. Short texts are not scored automatically.
  4. Single passage: select text and press Alt+Shift+C, or right-click a paragraph → "Check this paragraph for AI".
  5. Switch off with Alt+Shift+A or the toggle in the popup. Banking and mail sites are never scanned.
