AI Content Flag

Three quick steps. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is scanned until you click a button. Every step can be skipped and reopened later from the settings.

  1. 1 · Choose a model
  2. 2 · When to scan
  3. 3 · Try it

Choose a model

The model decides how fast and how accurate the scoring is. It runs entirely in your browser; the texts of the pages you read never leave the computer. Pick one – no model is selected for you.

Use my own server instead

Without a model nothing can be scored. The popup will link back here.

Choose when to scan

Websites are only looked at when this setting says so. Online banking, mail and pages with password or payment fields are never scanned automatically (blocklist, adjustable in the settings).

A hint, not proof:
  • Human texts get marked red too – especially factual, formulaic or very smoothly edited ones (encyclopedia articles, press releases, how-tos).
  • Not a probability. "AI score 97" does not mean "97% by an AI"; the number is not calibrated.
  • Not a judgment about people. Please do not accuse anyone of using AI on this basis alone.
  • English only, short texts are not scored automatically, revised AI text and newer models are detected less well.
