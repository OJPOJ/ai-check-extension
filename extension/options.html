Scores longer paragraphs of text with an AI-text classifier and marks them green / yellow / red. Scores are estimates – they can be wrong, especially for short, translated or heavily edited texts.

Detection

Where the texts are scored and with which model.

Text excerpts (up to 2000 characters per paragraph) are sent to . Use "Only on button press" or a site list if you do not want that for every site.

Display

From which score a paragraph is marked yellow or red.

not flagged unclear flagged (resembles AI text)
The score (0–100) is a raw value of the model, not a probability. Short paragraphs (under 120 words) are not marked yellow but gray "uncertain", and red only if the model has a stricter threshold for it (desklib: 98); paragraphs in languages the model does not know are not scored.

Scanning

When sites are checked automatically. You can check individual passages at any time via selection or right-click; Alt+Shift+S scans the current page.

One domain per line, subdomains are included.

Blocklist

Blocked sites are checked neither automatically nor via "Scan page now" – this takes precedence over all other settings. You can still deliberately check individual passages there via selection or right-click.

One domain per line, subdomains are included. Both also work via the popup ("Never scan here" / "Remove from blocklist").

Data & privacy

What the extension stores in this browser. None of it is synced or sent.

Paragraphs that have already been scored are marked immediately on the next visit instead of being computed again (or paid for again with cloud backends). Only a fingerprint (hash) of the paragraph is stored, with score, model and time – no text and no address. About 0.25 KB per paragraph – 30 days of heavy browsing add up to a few MB.

Feedback

The collection contains the text (up to 2000 characters), your answer, score, model and the language of the page – no address. It stays in this browser; the extension sends it nowhere. Exported as JSONL it serves as test material or training data (training/import_feedback.py). Entries "just my impression" are not used as a training label there by default.
