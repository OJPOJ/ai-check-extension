Traffic light

From which score a paragraph is marked yellow or red.

not flagged unclear flagged (resembles AI text)
The score (0–100) is a raw value of the model, not a probability. Short paragraphs (under 120 words) are not marked yellow but gray "uncertain", and red only for very high scores; paragraphs in languages the model does not know are not scored.

Scanning details

Blocklist

Blocked sites are checked neither automatically nor via "Scan page now" – this takes precedence over all other settings. You can still deliberately check individual passages there via selection or right-click.

One domain per line, subdomains are included. Both also work via the popup ("Never scan here" / "Remove from blocklist").

Remembered scores

Paragraphs that have already been scored are marked immediately on the next visit instead of being computed again (or paid for again with online backends). Only a fingerprint (hash) of the paragraph is stored, with score, model and time – no text and no address. About 0.25 KB per paragraph – 30 days of heavy browsing add up to a few MB.
