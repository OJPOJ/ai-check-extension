AI Content Flag

Marks longer paragraphs of text by how much they resemble AI-generated text. Before you start, the essentials in two minutes.

What the colors mean

AI score 97
Flagged – the paragraph strongly resembles what the model learned as AI text.
AI score 75
Unclear – some features, no clear tendency.
AI score 12
Not flagged – nothing typical found.
uncertain
Too short to tell – high score, but with so little text the model is wrong too often.
What the colors do not mean:
  • Not proof. Human texts get marked red too – especially factual, formulaic or very smoothly edited ones (e.g. encyclopedia articles, press releases, how-tos).
  • Not a probability. "AI score 97" does not mean "97% by an AI". The number is a raw value of the model and not calibrated.
  • Not a judgment about people. Please do not accuse anyone of using AI on this basis alone – for example at school, university or work.

Limits

  • English only. The shipped models only know English text. Paragraphs in other languages are therefore not scored; they can still be checked via right-click, but the result is then not meaningful.
  • Short texts (comments, captions, lists) are not scored automatically.
  • Revised AI text and newer models are detected less well.
